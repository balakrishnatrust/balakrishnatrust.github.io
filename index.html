Who We Are

About Us

Sri Bala-krishna Charitable Trust (Regd.) is a registered charitable trust founded in 2013 and based at Cox Town, Bengaluru. The trust was established with a vision to empower communities, support education, and promote environmental sustainability.

Our mission extends beyond charity — we build lasting partnerships with organizations and individuals who share our vision of a better world.

What Drives Us

Our Mission

We believe in creating measurable, sustainable impact through:

  • ✓ Empowering communities with access to quality education
  • ✓ Supporting healthcare initiatives for underserved populations
  • ✓ Promoting environmental conservation and sustainability
  • ✓ Building infrastructure that serves vulnerable communities
