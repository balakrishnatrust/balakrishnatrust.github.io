About Us

Sri Balakrishna Charitable Trust is a registered charitable trust dedicated to improving lives through meaningful community initiatives. Operating in Bangalore, we work tirelessly to empower communities, support education, and promote environmental sustainability.


Our mission extends beyond charity - we build lasting partnerships with organizations and individuals who share our vision of a better world.

Our Mission

We believe in creating measurable, sustainable impact through:


  • ✅ Empowering communities with access to quality education
  • ✅ Supporting healthcare initiatives for underserved populations
  • ✅ Promoting environmental conservation and sustainability
  • ✅ Building infrastructure that serves vulnerable communities

Our Impact Areas

📚

Education Support

Providing scholarships, learning materials, and educational infrastructure to underprivileged children.

💧

Water & Sanitation

Building clean water access and sanitation facilities in rural communities.

🌱

Environmental Conservation

Promoting sustainable green coverage, waste recycling initiatives, and community afforestation programs.
